There are to ways to move an ArmorStand:

## Option 1 - Command (Recommended)
First, to move an ArmorStand its better to do it with the command: `/as move <name>` which moves the armorstand to your location

## Options 2 - [ArmorStandMenu](armorstand-menu)
There is an other option to move an ArmorStand, but it is not recommended:

- Shift+RightClick on the ArmorStand to open the menu
- Shift+Click on the close item to open ToolsMenu
- Click `Move` 
- Break the block you want the armorstand to be on
